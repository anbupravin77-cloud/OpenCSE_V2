Content & Copyright Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Editorial Philosophy & Originality

OpenCSE is committed to publishing high-yield, structured, and pedagogical academic material for Computer Science engineering courses. Our curriculum structures follow standard university course outcomes (COs) and outcome-based education (OBE) rubrics.

2. AI Transparency & Human Review

While automated drafting tools may be used to structure outlines or format code snippets, every educational module undergoes human editorial oversight. We do NOT publish automated thin content, spam, or unverified claims.

3. DMCA & Copyright Takedown Procedure

OpenCSE respects the intellectual property rights of authors, professors, and educational publishers. If you believe any material hosted on OpenCSE infringes a valid copyright, please submit a takedown notice via our Contact Page or by emailing anbupravin77@gmail.com with:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  • The exact URL on OpenCSE where the material appears.
  • Your contact information (name, organization, email address).
  • A good-faith statement of non-authorization.

Verified takedown requests are processed within 48 business hours.
